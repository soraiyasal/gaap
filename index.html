Defining Purpose

We are not expanding where accountability happens. We are expanding who can participate in it.

That includes survivors, refugees, diaspora communities, journalists, and grassroots groups who cannot afford specialist legal support.

Strategic advice

Helping partners understand which accountability pathway fits their evidence and goals.

Investigations support

Practical assistance turning documentation into usable evidence.

Legal research

Specialist expertise most partner organisations cannot access alone.

Why We Exist

More legal avenues exist today than ever before. Most go unused.

01

Evidence goes unused

Many organisations document abuses. Few can assess which legal pathway is most likely to succeed.

02

Expertise is the obstacle

The barrier is rarely the law itself — it's the strategy and capacity to act on it.

03

Years of work, no route

Communities that spent years documenting abuses are left without a realistic path to justice.

We exist to close that gap — evidence into strategy, strategy into accountability.
Our Approach

"What is the most effective pathway to justice in this situation?"

The answer isn't always criminal prosecution — it may be sanctions, civil litigation, or another route. We help you find the right one.

Who We Support

Broader than most accountability organisations.

Those closest to injustice aren't always where the violations occurred. A border shouldn't make justice less accessible.

Survivors & survivor-led associations Refugee-led organisations Diaspora communities Families of the disappeared Grassroots documentation groups Journalists & investigators Civil society organisations Lawyers & legal clinics Universities & research institutions

People forced into exile remain central to this work for decades. We work with them, wherever they are.

What Makes Us Different

Most organisations specialise in one part of the process. We specialise in the space between them.

Rather than measuring success by reports published or events convened, we measure it by whether our work helps make meaningful accountability possible.

Guiding Principles

Five commitments that shape every engagement.

Accessibility

Understandable, navigable justice

Specialist legal expertise should not be available only to those with institutional resources or international connections.

Partnership

With — not instead of

We work alongside those already pursuing accountability. Our role is to strengthen existing efforts and build lasting capability.

Practicality

Focused on outcomes

Every engagement should leave partners better equipped to pursue accountability than before we became involved.

Independence

Guided by evidence and integrity

We remain independent of political parties, governments, and armed groups.

Long-Term Commitment

Accountability rarely happens quickly

We are committed to supporting sustained efforts that may take years to achieve meaningful results.

For Funders & Partners

Evidence that already exists is the highest-leverage investment in justice.

Years of testimony and evidence exist but never reach a mechanism capable of acting on it — not for lack of merit, but capacity. Funding that gap is one of the most efficient ways to convert existing human rights work into results.

Why funders work with us
  • Independent & non-partisan. Free of political parties, governments, and armed groups — decisions are led by evidence, not affiliation.
  • Capacity-building, not case-taking. Partners leave every engagement stronger, with skills and relationships that outlast our involvement.
  • Transparent reporting. Clear accounting of where support went and what pathway it advanced.
  • Reach beyond borders. Support for exile and diaspora-led work that most funding structures overlook.
